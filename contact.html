Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do you respond?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 2–3 business days. For urgent matters, please mention it in the subject line.

Do you accept guest posts or advertising?

We do not currently accept guest posts, sponsored content, or advertising. BenchReturn is a free, independent comparison tool.

Can I request a new index to be added?

Yes — we're always open to suggestions. Let us know which benchmark you'd like to see, and we'll evaluate it for future updates.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We don't store any personal data unless you explicitly send it to us via this form, and we never share it with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.
