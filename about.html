About Indexly

Built for clear market comparison

Why this tool exists, and who it's for.

What Indexly does

Indexly lets you compare the daily closing performance of major global stock market indices — including Nifty 50, S&P 500, NASDAQ, Shanghai, and DAX — across any time range, side by side.

Why daily closes

Markets move constantly during trading hours, but day-to-day comparison is most meaningful when measured close-to-close. Indexly intentionally avoids intraday or minute-by-minute data, focusing instead on the number that matters most for long-term comparison: where each index ended the day.

Who it's for

Indexly is built for anyone curious about how markets around the world are performing relative to each other — students, long-term investors doing their own research, or anyone who wants a quick visual answer to "how is my market doing compared to others?"

Not investment advice

Indexly is an informational and comparison tool only. Nothing on this site should be taken as financial or investment advice. Always do your own research or consult a qualified advisor before making investment decisions.
