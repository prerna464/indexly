Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information BenchReturn collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Overview

BenchReturn ("we," "our," or "the site") provides a free tool for comparing the historical performance of global stock market benchmarks. This page explains our approach to privacy in plain language.

Information we do not collect

BenchReturn does not require account creation, login, or any form submission. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifying information directly from visitors using the comparison tool.

Cookies and automated data collection

Like most websites, BenchReturn may use cookies and similar technologies through third-party services, including:

  • Google AdSense — used to display advertising on this site. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this or other websites. Visitors can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • Analytics services (if enabled) — such as Google Analytics, which may collect anonymized usage data such as pages visited, time on site, and general location (city/country level) to help us understand how the site is used. No analytics service currently active on this site collects personally identifying information.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites. You may opt out of certain Google ad personalization by visiting Google's Ads Policies page.

Third-party links

BenchReturn may link to external sources (such as data providers or financial publications). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Data accuracy disclaimer

Market data displayed on BenchReturn is provided for informational and comparison purposes only. While we aim for accuracy, we do not guarantee the completeness or correctness of any figures shown, and nothing on this site constitutes financial or investment advice.

Children's privacy

BenchReturn is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at benchreturn@rediffmail.com.
